# ChordFlask

**Explore your own music collection with chords.**

ChordFlask is a free, local-first Linux application for browsing audio and video,
analyzing selected tracks for chords and beats, and displaying the results in
sync while the music plays.

![ChordFlask screenshot](example-screenshot.png)

## What it does

- Browse your local MP3, MP4 and WebM collection
- Analyze only the tracks you are interested in
- Display chords and beats synchronized with playback
- Correct chords manually and keep those edits across reanalysis
- Transpose and export chord sheets
- Optionally generate and play synchronized stems
- Keep media and analysis completely local

ChordFlask is designed primarily for playing along, improvising and exploring
music you already own.
